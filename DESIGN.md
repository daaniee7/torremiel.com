## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a visual presence that conveys over 30 years of certified technical mastery, localized reliability in the Costa del Sol, and energetic commercial accessibility. The UI must project institutional competence alongside approachable urgency—balancing heavy industrial authority with seamless conversion pathways.

Drawing from modern **Corporate / High-Conversion Modernism**, the aesthetic avoids generic trade clutter by utilizing structured whitespace, assertive typographic scales, crisp slate structural cards, and deliberate high-contrast energetic triggers. The UI reassures homeowners, residential communities, and commercial enterprises while empowering direct phone calls, WhatsApp consultations, and solar estimation flows.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high contrast, safety certifications, and commercial clarity:
- **Primary Navy (`#0F1E36`, `#1A2B49`):** Represents technical rigor, engineering certification, structural grounding, and institutional trust. Used for dominant headers, primary structural bands, and authoritative badges.
- **Secondary CTA Amber (`#F59E0B`, hover `#EA580C`):** An energetic, electric warm amber-orange engineered strictly for conversion levers—calls to action, free quote triggers, and critical contact anchors.
- **Tertiary Solar Emerald (`#10B981`):** Applied exclusively to self-consumption features, eco-efficiency metrics, energy savings figures, and certified compliance stamps.
- **Neutral Framework (`#0F172A`, `#475569`, `#F8FAFC`, `#E2E8F0`):** Pure `#0F172A` Slate anchors high-hierarchy headlines; `#475569` handles body text with high legibility against the pristine `#FFFFFF` card layers and `#F8FAFC` slate canvas.
- **Urgent Accents:** `#DC2626` commands immediate attention on the 24h emergency banner without competing with standard commercial CTAs.

## Typography

The type hierarchy relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, utilizing its geometric yet approachable character to combine technical clarity with human warmth. 

- **Display & Headings:** Set with tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) and bold-to-extrabold weights to convey authority and commercial confidence. Desktop display scales step down smoothly on mobile layouts to prevent unwieldy wraps.
- **Body Text:** Minimum size is anchored at 16px (`body-md`) with generous line-height (`1.5` to `1.55`) to facilitate effortless reading for older demographics, property managers, and fast-moving site visitors.
- **Metrics & Trust Counters:** Rendered with tabular alignment, extra-bold weights, and amber or navy emphasis for immediate numerical proof.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid on desktop (`max-width: 1280px`), transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column layout on mobile.

- **Grid Distances:** Canvas outer margins dynamically compress from `2rem` (desktop) to `1rem` (mobile), while gutters shift from `1.5rem` to `1rem`.
- **Rhythm & Safe Areas:** Layout blocks prioritize clear vertical breathing rooms (`space-xl` between sections), enabling key conversion modules—such as the urgent contact bar, trust counters, and estimate requests—to stand out as distinct visual segments.
- **Floating Controls:** A dedicated vertical safe area of `80px` is preserved at the base of mobile screens to house persistent WhatsApp and click-to-call action anchors without obscuring copy or form buttons.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy utilizes a hybrid model combining **ambient diffused shadows** with crisp **low-contrast architectural outlines** (`1px solid #E2E8F0`):

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#F8FAFC` base canvas with zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Pure `#FFFFFF` background with `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.06), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.04)` and `#E2E8F0` hairline boundary.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Sticky Elements):** `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.03)`. Applied to sticky top navigation, active service cards, and elevated trust counters.
- **Level 3 (Floating Actions & Modals):** `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.06)` with subtle amber or navy ambient tinting for conversion drawers and floating communication nodes.

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced rounded architecture (Level 2). This provides a modern, friendly touch without sacrificing technical and industrial structure:
- **Base Components:** Inputs, buttons, and segmented pickers use `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Surface Cards & Accordions:** Structured with `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) or `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) for hero containers.
- **Pill Badges & Floating Knobs:** Badges, status markers, and mobile floating contact triggers use full pill rounding (`9999px`) to emphasize tap accessibility and distinct micro-interaction feedback.

## Components

### 1. Sticky Navigation & Emergency Strip
- **Top Utility Bar:** High-contrast `#DC2626` strip stating "Servicio de Urgencias 24h Benalmádena y Costa del Sol" with direct click-to-call link in white bold type.
- **Main Nav Header:** Fixed at viewport top; `#0F1E36` backdrop with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` fallback on scroll. Features high-legibility company mark, technical navigation links, and a prominent primary CTA button.

### 2. Buttons & Conversion Triggers
- **Primary CTA:** Energetic Amber (`#F59E0B`), text `#0F1E36` or `#000000` (800 weight), transitioning to `#EA580C` on hover. Elevated with subtle amber-tinted shadow on interaction.
- **Secondary Action:** Ghost navy border (`2px solid #0F1E36`), text `#0F1E36`, filling with `#0F1E36` and white text on hover.
- **WhatsApp Floating Button:** Positioned bottom-right, `#25D366` circular/pill container with white iconography and clear Spanish callout ("¿Hablamos por WhatsApp?").

### 3. Service & Solar Cards
- White surface, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `1rem` radius. Includes a colored micro-badge at top-left (`#10B981` for solar self-consumption, `#0F1E36` for industrial installation), high-contrast heading, concise scope description, and an inline link with chevron.

### 4. Trust Metric Counters
- Highlighting "+30 Años de Experiencia", "+1,500 Instalaciones Solares", and "Instaladores Autorizados". Displayed on `#F1F5F9` background with massive bold numbers in `#0F1E36` and secondary labels in `#475569`.

### 5. Input Fields & Lead Capture Forms
- `0.5rem` border-radius, `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #CBD5E1`. On focus: `2px solid #0F1E36` border with a `3px` outer ring in `#FBBF24` at 30% opacity. Clear labels in `label-md` with asterisks for required fields.

### 6. Accordions (FAQ & Technical Clarifications)
- Expandable panels for pricing, subsidies (NextGen solar funds), and emergency guarantees. Border-bottom `#E2E8F0`, bold question title, smooth rotational arrow icon, and clear typographic spacing for answer text.
